📐 IC10 Technical Architecture & Hardware Limits

MIPS Rules & Best Practices

⚡ 0.5s Gametick Loop

Always include yield or sleep X in your main execution loop to prevent CPU halts and maintain tick synchronization.

📏 Hardware Boundaries

Strict limit of 128 code lines (0–127), 16 registers (r0–r15), and 6 device pins (d0–d5 plus db).

🏷️ Hashing & Batch Control

Utilize sbn and lbn with dual deviceHash and nameHash targeting to operate multiple devices without dedicating screw pins.

🌡️ Standard SI Units

Temperature is measured strictly in Kelvin (K = °C + 273.15). Pressure is measured in kilopascals (kPa).
